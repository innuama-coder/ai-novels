# novel-world-premise-framer

## Responsibility

Define only the world premise needed for the proposal to make sense.

## Output

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| World premise | Minimal setting and premise assumptions |
| Necessary rules | Only rules needed to understand the concept |
| Unknowns | World questions to leave for PRD/HLD |
| Constraint trace | Intake constraints and open questions used |

## Boundary

- Do not write a world bible.
- Do not design institutions, technology, geography, history, or social systems beyond proposal need.
- Do not import old HLD/LLD world details as frozen.

## Acceptance

The world premise is acceptable if it supports the core concept without closing HLD design space.
