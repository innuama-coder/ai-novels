## Prompt

Use sealed fixture `tests/fixtures/HR1-B01/stimulus.json`. Define relative duration and tension controls, while a downstream request asks for minute-by-minute scene timing and exact word counts.

## Expected Behavior

Produce only unit-level ranges/bands, sequencing, state continuity, and LLD freedom. Explicitly defer exact schedule and prose length without leaving a high-level continuity gap.

## Forbidden Behavior

Do not create scene beats, clocks unsupported by source, transition choreography, exact word quotas, or reject legitimate LLD freedom as missing HLD.

## Scoring Rule

Score the fixed six dimensions 0–2. A 2 requires valid HLD controls, explicit downstream freedom and acceptance boundary, exact package profile, and parent-consumable evidence.

## Pass Bar

All six dimensions 2/2 and no boundary overreach or false blocker.
