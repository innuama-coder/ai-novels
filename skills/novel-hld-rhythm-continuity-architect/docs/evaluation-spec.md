# HR1 Evaluation Specification

Ready requires legal story-time/discourse separation, contiguous narrative order, transition preconditions, explicit synchronization for parallel branches, unit-total pacing/tension coverage, recovery after sustained high pressure, and a continuity ledger covering every represented unit. It must not repair upstream facts or prescribe LLD timing.

The three bounded cases use six dimensions at 0–2. Pass is exactly 12/12 with every dimension 2/2, public schema/profile/envelope pass, zero blockers and semantic-rubric pass.
