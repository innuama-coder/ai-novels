# HA1 research sources

Accessed: 2026-08-06. These sources inform the methodology; none overrides the
frozen HLD contract.

| # | Source | URL | Absorbed point |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aristotle, *Poetics*, Part VII (MIT Internet Classics Archive) | https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/poetics.1.1.html | A coherent whole needs non-arbitrary beginning, middle, end, causal succession, and graspable magnitude. |
| 2 | Aristotle, *Poetics*, Parts XII–XVIII (MIT) | https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/poetics.2.2.html | Complication, turning, and unraveling provide functional phase tests; episodes must remain relevant to the action. |
| 3 | Gustav Freytag, *Die Technik des Dramas* (Project Gutenberg) | https://www.gutenberg.org/files/50616/old/50616-h/50616-h.htm | Dramatic action is an ordered unity of moments; the model is historical technique, not a mandatory modern template. |
| 4 | Open University, *Approaching Prose Fiction* | https://www.open.edu/openlearn/mod/oucontent/view.php?id=1708&section=1 | Plot is the arrangement of story events, and structural choices should be justified by narrative purpose. |
| 5 | Open University, *Narrative and structural techniques* | https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/creative-writing/start-writing-fiction-duplicate?trackno=5 | Experienced novelists use varying structural techniques; form must serve the book being made. |
| 6 | Open University, *Creative writing and critical reading* | https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/creative-writing-and-critical-reading/content-section-3 | Structure, plotting, theme, POV, voice, and character are distinct review dimensions and should not be conflated. |
| 7 | MasterClass, *How to Structure a Story* | https://www.masterclass.com/articles/how-to-structure-a-story | Linear, nonlinear, parallel, circular, and interactive structures are alternatives; major events and perspectives constrain the choice. |
| 8 | MasterClass, *4 Ways to Structure Your Narrative Timeline* | https://www.masterclass.com/articles/ways-to-structure-your-narrative-timeline | Separate beats, story chronology, and plot arrangement; structure organizes turning points at whole-work scale. |
| 9 | Reedsy, *What Is Plot Structure?* | https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/plot/structure/ | Plot structure is ordered event progression; each stage has a function rather than a required percentage. |
| 10 | NY Book Editors, *A Guide to the Top Story Structures* | https://nybookeditors.com/2023/04/a-guide-to-the-top-story-structures/ | Structure selection depends on genre, audience, content, number of characters, and temporal organization; no framework is universally correct. |
| 11 | NY Book Editors, *How to Storyboard Your Novel* | https://nybookeditors.com/2022/04/how-to-storyboard-your-novel-and-why-you-should/ | Key moments can act as anchors for a big-picture plan; visual mapping exposes missing and imbalanced spans. |
| 12 | NY Book Editors, *How to Create Chapters in Your Novel* | https://nybookeditors.com/2017/09/your-guide-to-structuring-chapters/ | Chapters should organize known story and plot points around the reader's journey, supporting shells rather than dictating scene prose. |
