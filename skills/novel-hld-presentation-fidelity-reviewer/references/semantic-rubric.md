# Semantic rubric

## Pass anchors

- Reviewer and assembler task identities are distinct and the actual read set is frozen.
- Every report element resolves to current HP1 and states no more, less, or different than its sources.
- Every material HP1 control is represented and remains in the correct authority/open/LLD category.
- Visual nodes, edges, ordering, values, and labels agree with HP1 and the visual's textual conclusion.
- The independent author-visible budget matches the declared budget and is no more than twenty-four minutes.
- All nine report-only retrieval questions are accurately answered with HRE/HVG/source locators.
- Character and environment definitions are complete, source-faithful, mutually distinguishable, and understandable without consulting the conception package.
- Material drift and cognitive overload arrays are empty.
- Paraphrase and style differences with unchanged meaning do not become findings.

## Hard fail anchors

Any omission, weakening, enhancement, wrong attribution, changed relation/order/value/status, visual-data mismatch, budget overrun, material retrieval failure, or control hidden by process noise produces `fail/upstream_content` and prevents HH1-C.

## Reviewer-local revise

Use `revise/reviewer_local` only when the review's own evidence, challenge, binding, or test execution is incomplete or inconsistent. Do not disguise a report defect as reviewer-local revise.

## Out of scope

No score or finding may assess artistic quality, dramatic strength, realism, marketability, raw-conception fidelity, HTML layout, color contrast, keyboard support, or renderer implementation.

## Six-dimension release gate

P01/N01/B01 each require 2/2 for scope fidelity, input-gate obedience, output-contract correctness, semantic/deterministic correctness, boundary discipline, and parent consumability.
