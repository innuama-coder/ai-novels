# Semantic rubric

HF1 is script-only; acceptance is deterministic.

Pass only when:

- all 11 current upstream completions and their manifests/validations verify;
- author decision is external, current, unconsumed and exactly bound;
- run-state has no return or unresolved revision plan;
- HQ2 acceptance passes and current handoff hash agrees;
- approved alone creates completion and LLD admission evidence;
- revise targets resolve and are projected without interpretation;
- rejected produces author-rejected evidence only;
- package/result profiles and hashes are exact and deterministic;
- no output modifies HLD or contains plan/return/run-state/LLD content.

Any violated condition fails closed. A total score cannot compensate for a hard failure. P01/N01/B01 each require 2/2 in all six Harness dimensions.
