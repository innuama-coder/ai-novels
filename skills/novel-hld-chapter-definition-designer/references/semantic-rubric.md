# HD1 Semantic Rubric

## Pass anchors

1. Exact one-to-one HA1 shell coverage and exact HS1 storyline-role inheritance.
2. Every chapter has a distinct narrative necessity and material entry-to-exit change.
3. Goal, opposition, escalation, choice, cost, and consequence form a plausible causal unit; named or institutional choice actors and cost bearers are explicit, need not be the focal character, and are authorized for that chapter by HC1. Institutional actors retain exact source, responsibility, and authority limits.
4. POV and knowledge controls exactly bind the effective HI1 chapter assignment; time, place, pace, and continuity agree with HR1.
5. Character choices and relationship changes agree with HC1.
6. Required key functions and their completion conditions agree with HK1.
7. Reveal, withholding, clue, and open-question controls are explicit and fair.
8. Every memory chapter passes the complete nonlinear-memory standard and never treats memory alone as objective evidence.
9. Every adjacent chapter link carries an inherited change and unresolved pressure; no reset, filler, or arbitrary bridge chapter exists.
10. LLD handoff is sufficient, observable, and free of scene/prose realization.

## Blockers

Missing or duplicate shell; generic purpose; unchanged exit state; passive exposition without resistance or choice; unsupported character knowledge; POV that differs from the effective HI1 chapter assignment; unconscious or absent focal character; a decision attributed to the focal character merely because that character bears its cost; a public-service or collective decision coerced into an unrelated named character; unknown, implicit, or source-free institutional choice actor; misplaced reveal; continuity reset; memory used as fact; repeated chapter function; chapter that can be removed without consequence; LLD asked to decide missing HLD structure; prose/scene leakage; upstream rewrite; author-owned choice guessed locally.

## Verdicts

- `pass`: all chapter and chain anchors pass.
- `revise`: the HD1 definition, trace, specificity, or handoff needs local correction.
- `fail`: a sealed upstream model must change before a truthful chapter definition can exist.
