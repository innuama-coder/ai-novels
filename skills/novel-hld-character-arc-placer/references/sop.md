# HC1 SOP

## 1. Admit current structure and lines

Verify HC1 identity, ordered `[HB0, HA1, HS1]` completions, current hashes,
allowlist, and return binding. Resolve stable IDs through registered roles.

## 2. Assess unresolved dependencies

Create one `HDD-HC1-<source_id>` per projected source before any candidate.
Stop for external author resolution when disposition is `required_now`.

## 3. Freeze authority

For every core/important character and relationship, record authoritative
goals, values/beliefs, responsibilities, feelings, capabilities, permissions,
knowledge, beginning state, ending control, and open items. Mark absent facts as
out of scope; do not fill them.

## 4. Map participation

Walk each HS1 segment and connection. Assign an authorized responsibility or a
specific legitimate absence to each relevant character. Identify all key
results requiring an actor.

## 5. Generate arc-placement options

Compare at least two materially different legal placements, or document why
only one preserves event and relationship authority. Test agency, pressure,
choice, cost, consequence, line function, and downstream information needs.

## 6. Project arcs and relationships

Create state models; start, pressure, choice, cost, and result steps; bilateral
relationship steps; choice-cost-consequence links; event alignment; and
capability/information/permission checks. Bind each to existing IDs.

## 7. Bind chapter agency

Walk HA1 chapter shells in exact order. For each shell, publish the legal focal
characters, named and/or source-bound institutional decision actors, cost bearers, unavailable characters, and one
information path per legal focal character. Keep unconscious or absent people
out of focal and decision roles even when the chapter concerns their body,
rights, memories, or consequences. When emergency services, police, courts, or
another collective makes the consequential choice, preserve that role directly
with its chapter source, responsibility, and authority limit instead of
inventing a named participant or borrowing an unrelated character.

If the work order is a return resolution, load its sealed return binding before
drafting. For a participation target group, copy the affected
`HAS-PARTICIPATION-*` IDs as the exact ordered participation coverage and
verify each one still binds the corresponding `CHR-*` identity. For a chapter
agency target group, resolve every affected `HAS-AGENCY-*` ID to exactly one
chapter contract and preserve the sealed chapter order. For the supported
combined repair, resolve the ordered agency targets first and the ordered
`HAS-ARC-*` arc-step targets second, load the accepted HC1 return base, and
prove that every non-target semantic object is unchanged. Keep the authority
partition and every derivation claim's inventory/order/ID/target locator exact.
Only an existing `/chapter_agency_contracts` or `/arc_steps` claim may refresh
its source refs and value hash to prove the changed target collection; all
other claims remain exact and public source/hash validation still applies. Do
not mix participation with
another family, reverse the family order, reduce the list, substitute another
object, or generate new IDs.

Before publication, scan the participation register separately. Its
`responsibilities[]` may state only natural-Chinese role duties, and every item
must begin with `负责`. Remove
unexplained English prose; single-letter story names such as C, M, and R-07 are
allowed. Move every claim about death, survival, consciousness, recovery,
termination, or return into the relevant chapter-agency availability/constraint
field and resolve it against current HA1/HS1 continuity.
The runtime guard catches malformed duty sentences and explicit lifecycle
wording. During fresh semantic review, independently attack euphemisms, hidden
state claims, disguised design language, and stale-source conflicts; never
accept HC1 from producer validation alone. For every duty, ask whether the
person or institution could reasonably say “this is my job or decision” inside
the story. If the sentence instead explains suspense, conflict, plot movement,
failure paths, setup, payoff, or reader effect, rewrite it as a concrete
in-world goal, action, or authority boundary.

## 8. Adversarial review

Attack every change as a possible jump, author invention, plot compulsion,
unsupported action, one-sided relationship, or unowned result. Cite actual IDs
for every pass conclusion.

## 9. Publish

Run the deterministic entrypoint. Only its package and external result envelope
are consumable; Markdown and conversation are never canonical.
