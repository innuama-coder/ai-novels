# HS1 methodology

## Method

Model the novel as a network of story strands, not as a main plot with
decorative leftovers.

1. **Semantic extraction** — reconstruct actual lines from authoritative
   objects. Give each line a driver, goal, conflict, turn, payoff, scope, and
   authority status. Do not count inventory rows.
2. **Line integrity** — for every mandatory line, test establishment,
   development, turn, and payoff/open obligation. A line without change or
   consequence is background material, not a full strand.
3. **Network function** — classify each connection as causal, informational,
   emotional contrast, thematic mirror, relay, convergence, split, or payoff.
   A connection must change or illuminate at least one endpoint.
4. **Placement alternatives** — compare at least two segment/connection plans
   that differ in HA1 unit placement, connection type, activation, or payoff
   order. Examine orientation cost, interruption, line starvation, and
   convergence pressure.
5. **Main-trunk continuity** — prove the principal causal line is continuous
   from its authorized start through ending boundary, even when other strands
   temporarily dominate attention.
6. **Whole-story reconstruction** — remove each active line in turn. If the
   subject, causality, emotional meaning, or theme is unchanged, the line is
   probably redundant or misclassified. If all lines are read together, they
   must reconstruct one story rather than adjacent novellas.

## Semantic anchors

### Pass

- Mandatory lines have complete internal movement or explicitly preserved open
  payoff.
- Connections affect, illuminate, contrast, or complete other lines and cite
  both endpoints.
- Event placements preserve existing event facts and causal order.
- Optional lines are activated only when their contribution justifies cost.
- HC1 can identify participation duties without rebuilding the line network.

### Revise

Facts remain valid, but segment placement, connection design, switching,
activation, or payoff distribution needs HS1-local correction.

### Fail

The network requires a missing or changed conception storyline/event, or an
author-owned question must be answered before legal placement exists.

## Counterexamples

- Treating character, emotion, theme, and background labels as automatically
  separate full plots.
- Connecting two lines only because they appear in the same chapter.
- Padding an optional melody line until it competes with the trunk.
- Letting a line vanish after setup or return only as an unexplained payoff.
- Designing POV alternation or scene content in a storyline artifact.
