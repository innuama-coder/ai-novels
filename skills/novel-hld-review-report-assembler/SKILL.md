# Novel HLD Review Report Assembler

Operate as the bounded HH1-A hybrid skill. The harness owns orchestration, sealing, returns, and composite publication. This skill owns only the semantic organization of `hld-human-review-report.json`; HP1 remains the machine authority.

## Required reading

Read [input-output-contract.md](references/input-output-contract.md), [runtime-contract.json](references/runtime-contract.json), [sop.md](references/sop.md), [methodology.md](references/methodology.md), and [semantic-rubric.md](references/semantic-rubric.md) completely before execution. Use [sources.md](references/sources.md) for method provenance.

## Execution

1. Admit only stage `HH1`, exact owner `novel-hld-review-report-assembler`, ordered upstream `[HB0,HP1,HQ2]`, current HP1/HQ2 bindings, and the exact ordered allowlist in `runtime-contract.json`.
2. Create the exact bounded draft profile described by the runtime contract. Record actual reads as the complete ordered allowlist.
3. Read story content only from HP1. Use HQ2 only to prove admission; never use it as a content source.
4. Follow the SOP: inventory every material HP1 control plus the sealed conception world/character/place definitions, group them into the ten fixed HLD domains, order conclusions for author decisions, add only minimum-useful visual models, bind every display element, and compute the fixed review budget.
5. Preserve every canonical value and constraint strength. If HP1 lacks a required conclusion, emit durable blocked evidence returning to HP1; do not repair it.
6. Invoke the sole public entrypoint: `python scripts/run_skill.py <work-order.json> <draft-directory>`. It re-admits every sealed input, validates the draft, reproduces it twice, and atomically seals the exact internal package plus package-external `stage-result.json`.
7. Exit `0` means ready, `2` means durable blocked/revise, and `1` means a runtime failure. A ready result always has `result_scope=composite_internal`, no completion, and a non-null internal manifest.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not read raw conception, HA1-HK1 packages, prior reports, or chat.
- Do not read or generate a sample chapter or any novel prose. Sample prose belongs to a later development/presentation task and is a hard input-closure violation here.
- Do not create, correct, weaken, strengthen, or reinterpret a story fact, HLD decision, open item, prohibition, or LLD freedom.
- Do not expose candidates, reasoning chains, production notes, or rationale for how the Agent worked.
- Do not collapse a materially multi-city story into one “main city”. Define at least four cities with distinct event responsibilities and connected physical/information routes; ordinary-person portraits must cover every city.
- Do not delete a material control to meet ten minutes; reorganize or compress without semantic loss.
- Do not claim fidelity pass; HH1-B owns independent fidelity review.
- Do not generate HTML, SVG markup, CSS, DOM locators, or `HUI-` IDs; HH1-C owns rendering.
- Do not write run-state, publish top-level HH1 completion, or choose a final return owner.

## Done

Ready requires all ten HLD content domains; exactly five source-bound world-environment cards including a connected four-city narrative network and city-wide resident coverage; complete one-card-per-source character and concrete-place definitions; exact HRE/HVG bindings; no HUI IDs; a recomputed budget at or below eighteen minutes; exact internal package closure; and six evaluation dimensions at 2/2 for P01/N01/B01. Semantic fidelity remains subject to HH1-B.
