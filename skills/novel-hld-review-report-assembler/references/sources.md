# Research sources

Accessed 2026-08-06. Absorption points are implemented in the methodology and SOP; sources inform presentation mechanics, not novel content.

1. [ONS, Structuring content](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/content/writing-for-users/structuring-content) — front-load conclusions, descriptive headings, short self-contained chunks, inverted-pyramid order.
2. [CDC Clear Communication Index](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/ccindex.html) — make the main message obvious; assess language, visuals, and layout together.
3. [CDC Clear Communication Index User Guide](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/pdf/clear-communication-user-guide.pdf) — align words and visuals, label visuals, avoid overload.
4. [CDC NIOSH, Using Clear Communication](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/bulletin/2017/clear-communication.html) — important information first, chunk content, use instructive visuals near related text.
5. [CDC NCEH, Focus on Format and Layout](https://www.cdc.gov/nceh/clearwriting/mod3/index.html) — use chunks and white space to avoid walls of text and buried conclusions.
6. [W3C WAI, Technique G103](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G103) — use diagrams for complex concepts and require equivalent text.
7. [W3C WAI, Alternative Content for Complex Information](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o7p02-alternative-content/) — provide summaries and charts as complementary routes to complex information.
8. [UK Government Analysis Function, Data visualisation: charts](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/data-visualisation-charts/) — state the chart message, reduce clutter, annotate interpretation.
9. [UK Government Analysis Function, Communicating quality, uncertainty and change](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/communicating-quality-uncertainty-and-change/) — state what can and cannot be concluded; preserve uncertainty and prevent overinterpretation.
10. [U.S. GAO, Guide for Writing Executive Summaries](https://www.gao.gov/products/130024) — create a concise decision layer for readers who will not consume the full technical report.
11. [U.S. GAO, Evidence-Based Policymaking Practices](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105460) — bind conclusions to relevant evidence and make evidence usable by decision makers.
12. [Stanford Visualization Group, Narrative Visualization](https://vis.stanford.edu/files/2010-Narrative-InfoVis.pdf) — combine narrative order, visual structure, and annotations without replacing evidence.
13. [Nielsen Norman Group, How People Read on the Web](https://www.iso.org/files/live/sites/isoorg/files/styleguide/resources/How_People_Read_on_the_Web.pdf) — scanning follows descriptive headings; use a layer-cake hierarchy.
14. [UK HSE, Full report framework](https://www.hse.gov.uk/foi/internalops/og/ogprocedures/investigation/reportframewk.htm) — answer the scoped question directly, separate facts from assumptions, and do not exceed remit.
