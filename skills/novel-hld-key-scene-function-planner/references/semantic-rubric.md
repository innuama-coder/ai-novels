# HK1 Semantic Rubric

## Verdicts

- `pass`: all applicable anchors pass with object evidence and no actionable finding.
- `revise`: only HKF definition, placement, merge/split, coverage, LWR grouping, or evidence needs local correction.
- `fail`: an upstream structure, line, arc, information, time-space, continuity, source fact, or author answer must change.

## Pass anchors

1. Applicable high-level obligations equal covered obligations; dispositions account for legitimately inapplicable/open/deferred items.
2. Every HKF has one primary function, clear preconditions/trigger, material exit-state change, necessity proof, and completion criteria.
3. Secondary functions are compatible and do not obscure the primary function.
4. Merge/split choices pass time, place, participant, POV, knowledge, state, tension, and causal tests.
5. Key choices, revelations, payoffs, climax, and consequences are not resolved by convenience or unauthorized means.
6. Every HKF has an LWR consumer; every LWR has a non-arbitrary grouping rationale and sufficient entry/exit and acceptance.
7. LLD freedoms are real implementation choices and do not hide missing HLD decisions.
8. Authority/open items remain intact; upstream models are unchanged.
9. No LLD or prose detail appears.
10. The external result envelope gives the parent one deterministic next action.

## Blockers

Uncovered mandatory obligation; redundant/filler HKF; multiple competing primary functions; incompatible merged functions; arbitrary fixed count; missing HKF↔LWR consumption; one-line/coincidental/unauthorized/tool-character solution; upstream rewrite; `required_now` after candidate; ownership conflation; LLD/prose leakage; mixed package/result/completion.

## Counterexample anchor

An HKF says “the analyst explains the entire conspiracy, ending the crisis,” although no upstream information node gives that analyst the knowledge and no prior resistance remains. This is an unauthorized one-line solution, not a valid key-scene function.
