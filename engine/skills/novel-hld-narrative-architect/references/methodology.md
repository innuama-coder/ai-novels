# HA1 methodology

## Method

Treat structure as a causal, state-changing coordinate system rather than a
template. Build it from the approved story's obligations in six layers:

1. **Whole and magnitude** — identify the smallest complete span from valid
   beginning conditions through the authorized ending boundary. Each part must
   contribute to the whole, and the whole must remain cognitively tractable.
2. **Causal phase changes** — derive phases from changes in goal, pressure,
   option space, commitment, or consequence. A phase without a material state
   change is not a phase.
3. **Alternative forms** — compare linear, parallel, framed, circular,
   nonlinear, or story-specific hybrids by their ability to carry the actual
   events and constraints. Three acts are a candidate, never a default answer.
4. **Nested coordinates** — place phase, narrative unit, and chapter shell in a
   strict containment tree. Chapter shells are capacity containers, not scene
   summaries.
5. **Anchor and transition stress test** — place inciting disruptions,
   commitments, reversals, convergence, climax, consequence, and ending
   controls; then prove entry → change → exit continuity at every unit.
6. **Elasticity without vagueness** — express chapter density and length as
   bounded ranges with conditions for split/merge. Preserve LLD freedom while
   eliminating high-level guesswork.

## Selection criteria

Score candidates by obligation coverage, causal continuity, capacity for all
mandatory lines, ending fidelity, cognitive orientation, reversibility, and
downstream usefulness. Reject any candidate that requires a new major event,
changes an event's cause or outcome, leaks POV/reveal decisions, or describes a
scene instead of a container.

## Semantic anchors

### Pass

- Every unit changes an explicit state and hands a compatible state to its
  successor.
- Existing major events and ending controls have unambiguous anchors.
- The selected form demonstrably fits the story better than rejected forms.
- HS1 can place every line without inventing a new coordinate system.

### Revise

The story facts remain valid, but unit boundaries, anchor placement, capacity,
or structural choice need HA1-local correction.

### Fail

The structure can work only by changing conception authority, or a projected
author question is required before a candidate can exist.

## Counterexamples

- Renaming setup/confrontation/resolution as three custom phases without
  deriving them from story-state changes.
- Putting dialogue, actions, sensory beats, or POV choices into chapter shells.
- Declaring an open item irrelevant to the whole HLD; HA1 may only assess its
  own stage.
- Creating an event to repair an empty middle instead of returning the missing
  conception fact.
