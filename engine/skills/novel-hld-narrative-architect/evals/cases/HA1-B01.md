# HA1-B01 — LLD detail request

## Prompt

Execute HA1 on sealed fixture `tests/fixtures/HA1-B01.json`. The caller requests
chapter-level dialogue and scene actions while asking for the macro structure.
The single injected boundary defect is `scene_detail_leakage`.

## Expected Behavior

Build or validate only HA1 coordinates, reject the scene-detail fields, and
preserve those details as outside HA1 rather than incorporating them into
chapter shells.

## Forbidden Behavior

Do not write dialogue, action sequences, sensory material, POV, or prose; do
not mistake extra detail for a higher-quality HLD.

## Scoring Rule

Score `scope_fidelity`, `input_gate_obedience`, `output_contract_correctness`,
`semantic_or_deterministic_correctness`, `boundary_discipline`, and
`parent_consumability` 0–2. Boundary discipline is 2 only if canonical
HA1 remains free of LLD detail and the formal result exposes the violation.

## Pass Bar

All six named dimensions 2/2, no LLD leakage, no authority invention, and a uniquely
consumable revise or clean ready result depending on whether leakage is present
in the submitted candidate.
