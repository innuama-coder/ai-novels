# novel-macro-structure-designer

## Responsibility

Design the whole-novel macro structure at proposal granularity.

## Input

- Intake boundary ledger
- Accepted storyline and storyline quality report
- Final reader promise
- Final core concept

## Output

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Structure model | Single work, parts, volumes, acts, or other macro form |
| Movement logic | High-level beginning, middle, end function derived from the accepted storyline |
| Conflict escalation | How the intake conflict seeds intensify across the macro movement |
| PRD candidates | Relative independent portions suitable for PRD |
| Constraint trace | Intake constraints, conflict seeds, and open questions used |

## Rules

- Structure must be derived from the accepted storyline, not from old drafts.
- Each macro part must have a conflict function and escalation role.
- PRD candidates must be relatively independent and above scene level.
- Preserve final reader promise and final core concept.

## Boundary

- Do not write trunk scenes, chapter lists, or scene-by-scene beats.
- Do not import old PRD/HLD structure as binding.
- Do not decide world mechanics unless needed for macro logic.
- Do not create a structure that merely labels parts without escalating conflict.
- Do not reorder, replace, or weaken the accepted storyline's main causal chain, focal character rule, Terminal agency map, human motive anchor, or terminal outcome.
- Do not choose a macro shape that contradicts the final reader promise or final core concept.

## References

Good macro structure:

- Shows beginning, middle, and ending movement as pressure changes.
- Gives PRD a controlled scope without prewriting scenes.
- Makes conflict escalation visible across parts.

Bad macro structure:

- Is only act labels, chapter titles, or aesthetic rhythm.
- Reorders the story to fit a template.
- Turns PRD candidates into trunk scene lists.

## Traceability

Each macro part and PRD candidate must trace to accepted storyline segments, conflict seeds, final reader promise, and final core concept.

## Acceptance

The structure must let PRD choose a controllable scope without prewriting PRD, and each macro part must have a clear conflict function that preserves the accepted storyline, final reader promise, and final core concept.
