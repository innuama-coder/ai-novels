defect is `unauthorized_new_relationship` between an existing character and a
person not authorized in the baseline.

and publish local-candidate revise or the appropriate upstream-content blocked
evidence without completion. Do not repair the relationship in HC1.

motivation, or publish ready because the rest of the arc is coherent.

## Scoring Rule

Score `scope_fidelity`, `input_gate_obedience`, `output_contract_correctness`,
`semantic_or_deterministic_correctness`, `boundary_discipline`, and
`parent_consumability` 0–2. Semantic correctness requires comparing the
relationship claim to authority, not merely matching a forbidden key.

## Pass Bar

non-ready result is uniquely parent-consumable and contains no completion.
