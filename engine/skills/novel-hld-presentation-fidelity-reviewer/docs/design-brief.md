# Design brief

## Problem

HH1-A deliberately transforms machine-oriented HP1 controls into a concise human review layer. That transformation can omit, weaken, enhance, misattribute, reorder, or visually distort material information. Self-review would not provide independent evidence.

## Outcome

HH1-B produces a sealed, source-grounded fidelity review proving whether the report preserves HP1 and supports seven fixed author retrieval tasks within ten minutes.

## Scope

The skill reviews presentation fidelity and decision usability only. It does not review story quality, conception fidelity, HTML rendering, visual styling, or author preference. It does not edit either compared artifact.

## Success

Every report element traces to HP1, every material HP1 control is represented without changed authority, visual-model data are identical to their sources, the budget recomputes, retrieval answers use only report elements, findings are empty, and reviewer/assembler identities are distinct.
