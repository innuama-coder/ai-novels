# Evaluation Rubric

## Reviewer role

An independent reviewer verifies both structural evidence and whether the
outputs preserve author intent, causal coherence, world constraints and genuine
character agency.

## Dimensions

Score each 0–2: scope fidelity, workflow rigor, evidence provenance, validation
hardness, semantic completeness, adapter faithfulness and release portability.

## Blockers

Block direct stage bypasses, unapproved plan changes, missing question-answer
pairs, hidden uncertainty, a self-recorded log presented as event-backed, or a
downstream artifact that omits the required completion packet.

## Pass bar

No blocker and at least 12/14. A 10–11 can be `pass_with_risks` only when risks
are explicitly accepted by the author; anything else is `revise` or `blocked`.
